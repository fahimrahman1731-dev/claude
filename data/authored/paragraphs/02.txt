=== p004 | Life Along the River | geography | E
Many towns in Bangladesh grew up next to a river. The water is used for drinking, cooking and growing food in the fields. Boats carry rice and other goods from one town to another, and fishermen sell their catch at the market every morning. In the past, roads were poor, so the river was the main way to travel. Today there are bridges and highways, but the river is still important for daily life. Children swim near the banks when the weather is hot. For many families, the river is part of their home.

=== p005 | The Human Heart | the body | E
The heart is a strong muscle that works all day and all night. It is about the size of a closed hand and sits in the middle of the chest. The heart has four parts, and small valves open and close to keep blood moving in the right direction. Blood goes to the lungs to pick up fresh oxygen and then travels around the body. When you exercise, your heart beats faster because your muscles need more energy. When you sleep, it slows down and gets some rest between beats. A healthy diet keeps this hard worker strong.

=== p006 | A Rainy Day at Home | story | E
On Saturday morning, it started to rain very hard. My little brother wanted to play football, but the park was full of water. Instead, we sat on the floor and built a tower with old books and boxes. Then our grandmother taught us a card game that she played as a child. She laughed when we made mistakes, and soon we were laughing too. In the afternoon, the rain stopped and a rainbow appeared over the houses. It was one of the best days of the summer.

=== p007 | My First Day at a New School | story | E
I was very nervous on my first day at the new school. The building was big, and I did not know anyone in my class. My teacher smiled and asked a girl named Rina to show me around. She showed me the library, the science lab and the place where we eat lunch. At break time, she introduced me to her friends, and they asked me many questions about my old town. By the end of the day, I was not afraid any more. I walked home feeling happy and excited for tomorrow.

=== p008 | The Village Bakery | business | E
There is a small bakery at the end of our street. The owners start work at four o'clock every morning to bake fresh bread and cakes. They use simple ingredients, such as flour, butter, eggs and sugar, but their recipes are very old. People come from other parts of the city because the bread is so good. The bakery is always busy on weekends, and there is often a line outside the door. The owners know most of their customers by name. For many people, a visit to the bakery is part of their daily routine.

=== p009 | Staying Healthy | health | E
There are many simple ways to stay healthy. First, try to walk or ride a bike for at least thirty minutes every day. It is also important to drink enough water, especially when the weather is hot. Fresh fruit and vegetables give your body the vitamins it needs to grow and fight illness. Sleep is just as important as food, so go to bed at the same time each night. If you feel tired or sick, rest and ask a doctor for advice. Small habits can make a big difference to your health.

=== p010 | A Walk in the Forest | nature | E
Last spring, my class went on a trip to a forest near the city. We walked along a narrow path under very tall trees. Our guide showed us the nest of a small bird and the footprints of a deer in the soft mud. We also saw colorful flowers and many kinds of insects. At lunch time, we sat by a stream and listened to the sound of the water. The guide told us that the forest is home to thousands of animals and plants. Everyone agreed that we must protect it for the future.

=== p011 | The Lost Cat | story | E
One evening, our cat did not come home for dinner. We looked for her in the garden and under all the cars on our street. My sister made a poster with a photo of the cat and put it on the wall of the shop. The next morning, a neighbor knocked on our door. She said that she heard a noise in her garage and found our cat sleeping in an old box. We were so happy and thanked her many times. Now we always check the garage before we close the door.

=== p012 | Festival Season | culture | E
Every year, the city holds a big festival in the spring. The streets are full of music, food and bright colors. Local artists sell paintings and crafts, and families come from nearby villages to join the fun. In the evening, there are dances and songs on a large stage in the main square. Many people wear traditional clothes, and children eat sweets until late at night. The festival brings the whole community together. When it ends, everyone is already waiting for next year.

=== p013 | How Plants Grow | science | E
Every plant starts as a small seed in the soil. When the seed gets water and warmth, it begins to grow roots that go down into the ground. Soon a small green stem pushes up toward the light. The leaves use sunlight, water and air to make food for the plant, and this process also gives off oxygen. As the plant grows taller, it needs more space and more water. Some plants make flowers, and bees carry pollen from one flower to another. In this way, new seeds are made and the cycle begins again.

=== p014 | Morning Market in the Old City | city life | E
The old city wakes up early, long before the shops on the main road open. By six o'clock, the morning market is already busy and noisy. Farmers bring fruit, fish and vegetables from the countryside in small trucks and on bicycles. Buyers walk slowly between the stalls and check the prices before they choose. Some sellers shout to get attention, while others wait quietly and drink tea. By nine o'clock, most of the fresh fish is gone. The street is quiet again when the sun is high.

=== p015 | Learning to Ride a Bicycle | story | I
When I was eight years old, my father decided to teach me to ride a bicycle. We went to an empty parking lot on a Sunday morning, when there were no cars. At first, he held the back of the seat while I pushed the pedals very slowly. I fell several times and hurt my knee, but he told me not to give up. After an hour, I looked back and realized that he was no longer holding the bike. I was riding by myself across the whole parking lot. I still remember how proud I felt that day.
